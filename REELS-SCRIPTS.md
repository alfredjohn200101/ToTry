## TO TRY — 12 SHOOTABLE SCRIPTS

Companion to `CREATOR-PLAYBOOK.md`. That file governs *why* and *when* you publish; this one is the *what to point the camera at*. Same rules apply here: past tense, reps not resolutions, scars not wounds, and every video useful even to someone who never installs anything.

**Before you shoot anything (read once, then forget it):**
- iPhone screen record: Settings → Control Centre → add Screen Recording. Long-press the button → mic ON if you're talking over it, OFF for the silent ones.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb first. A notification banner mid-take kills the take.
- Talking-head bits: phone against a wall at chest height, one window to your side, stand 60cm back. That's the whole lighting rig.
- Record voiceover separately in Voice Memos while walking around your room. You'll sound like a person instead of a presenter.
- Do the screen actions **slowly** — about 30% slower than you'd naturally tap. Viewers can't follow your thumb.
- One take is allowed to be the take. Being a beginner on camera is the trust; polish is what people distrust.
- Every script below is written so nothing on screen is invented — these are the real labels, real copy, real buttons, **re-verified against v667 on 28 Oct 2026**.

**Before you shoot — the app's own state (added 28 Oct 2026, every line measured on v667):**
- **Turn Reduce Motion OFF** (Settings → Accessibility → Motion). With it on, sheets stop sliding and *pop* — measured 0.42s → 0.00001s — so script 1's "sheet slides up" doesn't happen. Guided breathing still animates either way; it's deliberately exempt.
- **Two sheets can open on their own, 1.2s and 2.6s after launch** — "How are you, really?" and the hard-hour greeting. Neither can fire if you have no urge logged in the last 3 hours, no slip in the last 12, and you're outside the block the app has learned is your risk window. If you're not sure: open the app, dismiss whatever appears, close it, then open it again for the real take. The first open stamps both.
- **Don't film signed out.** In guest mode the Release screen adds a "Keep this →" button and a sign-up line under it — in frame, on the one reel whose whole argument is that no engagement app could ship that screen.
- **Check Settings → About you has your name on it.** "What are you feeling, Alfy?" and "Now put me down, Alfy." both read it live. Blank, and they render without the name.
- **The orb doesn't pulse any more.** It's a flat iOS-blue circle with a white speech-bubble icon in it, bottom-right. Don't hold the frame waiting for a breath — there isn't one.
- **The emoji are gone from rows and chips.** Every row is a coloured stroke-icon tile now. Any line below that lists 🫁 💬 ✎ 🌄 🔒 is describing v367, not what you'll see.
- **Scripts 6 and 7 put real bank data on camera.** The found-subscriptions card prints raw merchant strings straight from your statement.
- **The app counts your rehearsals.** Tap the same feeling three times and it names the pattern out loud; the plan row relabels itself "Make" → "Update" once one is saved. Rehearse on a *different* feeling than the one you film.

## 1 · THE ONE BUTTON
**Format: silent screen-record, text only (no voice at all)**

**HOOK (0–2s)**
Spoken: *nothing.* This one is silent on purpose.
On-screen text: **"I put one button on every screen. It isn't a menu."**
Visual: home screen, thumb resting near the small blue orb bottom-right — a flat iOS-blue circle with a white speech-bubble icon in it. It does **not** pulse any more, so hold the frame still for two beats instead of waiting for a breath.

**THE PROBLEM**
It's 11pm, you feel something you can't name, and your thumb is already on Instagram before you've noticed you're upset.

**SCREEN ACTIONS**
- 0–3s — Home. Hero, the **NEXT** row under it, and the top of the **"Your life, woven"** card below that. ‹ **The woven card does NOT fit in the first frame** — measured with the inset a real iPhone actually has, it starts at 483px and runs 558px, so about **two thirds reads at 393×852 and four fifths at 440×956**, and the bottom two life rows are below the fold. Frame for the eyebrow and the count, not the whole card. › Orb sitting still bottom-right.
- 3–6s — Tap the orb. Sheet slides up: **"What are you feeling, Alfy?"** — it reads your saved name live, so check Settings → About you before the take; blank, and it renders with no name at all. ‹ With **"Alfy"** it is ONE line at both phone sizes. It only wraps to two with a longer name like "Alfred" — which is what the test seed writes, not what your phone says. ›
- 6–12s — **No scroll needed — all ten chips fit, two to a row.** Hold on the full sheet and let the eye travel them: The pull · Restless / Flat · Anxious / Heavy · Heartache / Avoiding · Can’t start / Fired up · Actually good. Pause a half-beat on "Can’t start — frozen, staring at it".
- 12–17s — Tap **Restless — can’t settle, antsy**. Card: **"Restless energy"** — *"That buzz wants OUT, not in. Don’t scroll it away — that just traps it."*  ‹ **Rehearse on a different feeling.** This card counts your taps: the third tap of Restless adds a line naming the pattern, so a rehearsed take and the real one won’t match. ›
- 17–23s — **No pan needed either — the whole card fits**, exactly like the sheet before it (measured: it does not scroll at either phone size, and "Not now" sits 74px clear of the bottom). Let the eye travel it. **No emoji — each row is a coloured stroke-icon tile.** **Log a quick workout** (the blue button), then one grouped list: **Take a breath first** (teal, and it states its own mechanism underneath) · **Just talk** · **Write it down** (orange) · **Go outside and look up** (yellow) · **Make my plan for next time** (indigo padlock).
- 23–29s — Tap **Make my plan for next time**. One continuous take, no backing out, no second tap.

**VOICEOVER**
None. Silence is the point of this one.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**

2. "It asks one question."
3. "Ten answers. No typing."
4. "It doesn't log the feeling. It moves you out of it."
5. "And you can write the plan while you're calm…"
6. "…so next time, it hands you back your own words."

**THE TURN**
At 29s, when his own sentence comes back to him. Every other app would have saved the *feeling* and shown him a chart of it next month. This saved the *decision* and gave it back at the exact moment he'd have forgotten it.

**CTA**
On-screen, small, last frame: *"Free. Link in bio if you want it. If you don't — write your own version on a bit of paper. Same mechanism."*

**CAPTION**
No voiceover on this one because I want you to just watch the thing. The whole app has one button on every screen and it asks what you're feeling, not what you did. Ten answers, no typing — because nobody types when they're in it. Write your plan while you're clear-headed and it reads it back to you when you're not. That's it. That's the trick.

## 2 · THE APP THAT TELLS YOU TO PUT IT DOWN
**Format: problem-then-demo**

**HOOK (0–2s)**
Spoken: *"Every app on your phone is built so you don't leave. I built the ending in on purpose."*
On-screen text: **"THE ONLY SCREEN I'M PROUD OF"**
Visual: you, close, phone in hand, screen facing away from camera.

**THE PROBLEM**
A phone has no last page — no credits, no bottom — so you never actually finish. You just eventually stop.

**BEFORE THE TAKE (this script only — the universal rules are at the top of the file):**
- **Film this on a SUBSTANCE vice** — a name containing smok/cig/tobac/nicotine/vape/weed/cannabis/drink/alcohol/booze/beer/wine — set to an abstinence goal, with a cost recorded under Manage, at stage "Ready", and at least one clean day. A behaviour vice shows a shield row about self-respect where the $ row should be; a gambling vice opens a different screen headed "Before you put it on".
- **Sign in.** In guest mode the Release screen adds a "Keep this →" sign-up button and a line under it — in frame, on the one reel whose whole argument is that no engagement app could ship that screen.
- If somebody is named in your reach-out list and not held quietly, the Release screen offers a quiet second button, *"…or go message <name>"*. That is the app pointing beyond itself, in shot — use it or avoid it on purpose, but know it is there.
- **Four things on the 8–14s screen render conditionally — set them up or they are simply absent:** the gold **"YOUR PLAN — IN YOUR WORDS"** card needs a plan saved on that vice (Manage → its "why" and "your move"), or it returns nothing; **"came back N times"** only appears once that count is above zero; the **reframe** line can be empty, and the exact sentence quoted here is the hardest of its three forms to get — it needs that morning’s ritual completed with an identity line in it.
- **The Fight tab’s lede has three forms and one of them is silence.** *"You stayed in it N of the last 14 days."* is the form that needs a LOGGED SLIP in the window. With none, you get a different sentence or none at all — so check what is actually on screen before you roll.
- The **Nh Nm** on the vice card is time elapsed *within today*, not total hours, so it will read small beside the day count. That is correct, not a bug.

**SCREEN ACTIONS**
- 0–4s — Talking head, one line.
- 4–8s — Cut to screen. Fight tab. **The 48px "DAYS CLEAN" clock with ticking seconds is gone — deliberately deleted, and what replaced it is a better shot.** Large title **Fight**, then the lede: *"You stayed in it N of the last 14 days."* Scroll one screen to the vice card: big number **"Day N of the fight"** at 58px, and under it *N days clean · came back N times · Nh Nm* — the clock still ticks, hours and minutes, no seconds. A number that never resets, beside a streak that does.
- 8–14s — Tap the red button on the vice card, **"I’m feeling it — come here first"** → **"You came here first"** (no full stop). First the gold card **"YOUR PLAN — IN YOUR WORDS"** with his own words quoted back, then the reframe (*"This morning you said who you’d be today: ‘…’. This is the exact moment that meant."*). Then the stakes rows — **gold stroke icons, not emoji**: *[leaf] N days clean. This is the moment that keeps it, or ends it — nothing else does.* · *[wallet] $X already reclaimed by not buying. Falling now spends the streak AND restarts the meter.* · *[bulb] What’s this really about right now — tired, bored, stressed, alone? The urge is usually managing something else. Name that, and it loosens.*
- 14–19s — Tap **"The wave’s passing — I’m good"**. ‹ Curly apostrophes and **no full stop** on the real button — the script’s straight-quoted version with a period is not what is on screen. ›
- 19–27s — Full screen goes dark green-black. 🕊️ **"You came here instead."** Then: *"The wave passed — and you didn’t. Not falling was the whole thing; the feeling goes, this is what stays."* Then a small box, **ONE SMALL REAL THING** — *"When I put this down, I’ll [go for a walk]."* Then: *"Now put me down, Alfy. Go live the next hour — the real one, off the glass. I’m right here if the wave comes back. But you don’t need me right now."*
- 27–32s — Tap **"I'm putting it down."** Screen: **"Go well."** — fades to nothing. Phone goes in pocket, on camera.
- 32–38s — **The Release overlay leaves you on the FIGHT tab, so this needs one more tap: bottom bar → Today** (the first button; there is no control labelled "Home" — the five read Today · Fight · Grow · Money · Soul). Scroll to the **"Your life, woven"** card. Under its eyebrow sits a big count at 52px — **"N woven days"** — and beneath it the headline *"N times, you came here and walked back into your life."*  ‹ **Those are two different numbers and they will not match.** The 52px one counts tended days; the headline counts releases. Don’t write a graphic that assumes they agree. ›  ‹ The Release screen carries the fuller version of the sentence and it is the better-written one: *"N times now, you’ve come here and walked back into your life instead of down the glass."* ›

**VOICEOVER**
"Every other app is measured by how long you stay. So I built the opposite — the only number this thing is proud of. Not time in the app. The times you came in, sorted yourself out, and left. It says it out loud: put me down, go and live the next hour. And then it actually goes away. No 'what's next.' No feed underneath. That's the whole screen."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**
- "no last page = you never stop"
- "so I built one"
- "'Now put me down.'"
- "the only metric it keeps: times you LEFT"

**THE TURN**
"Go well" fading to a blank screen. No engagement app can ship that frame — it's revenue walking out the door. That's the proof the incentive is different, and you don't have to argue for it, you just show it.

**CTA**
"It's free and there's nothing to buy in it, so I've got no reason to keep you here. If you take one thing from this: decide in advance what the *end* of your phone session looks like. It won't happen by itself."

**CAPTION**
Adam Alter's line stuck with me — a phone has no last page, so we never put it down. So I built a last page. When you get through a hard moment in To Try it doesn't congratulate you and drop you into a feed; it tells you to put the phone down and go live the next hour, then it disappears. The only number it counts is how many times you left. Anti-engagement isn't a bug in this one.

## 3 · WHY I REFUSED TO PAYWALL THE SKILLS

## 4 · "URGES LAST 15 MINUTES" — WHERE THAT ACTUALLY COMES FROM
**Format: myth-correction**

**HOOK (0–2s)**
Spoken: *"You've heard cravings last 15 to 20 minutes. That number isn't from a study."*
On-screen text: **"15–20 MINUTES"** with a line struck through it.
Visual: you, mid-sentence, no intro. Straight in.

**THE PROBLEM**
You white-knuckle to twenty minutes, it's still there at twenty-five, and now you think you're the exception — so you fold.

**BEFORE THE TAKE (this script only):**
- **Your struck-through "15–20 MINUTES" and the app’s sentence say different numbers.** The app says 15–30, and calls it *"a clinical rule of thumb, not a measured law — yours may be shorter or longer."* Your line is about the number people *hear*, which is 15–20, so both are defensible — but both will be on camera in the same video. Pick one on purpose: either the graphic reads 15–30 to match the screen, or you say out loud that the internet says 20 and the clinical figure is 15–30. Your words, your call.
- **FIXED IN THE APP on 28 Oct 2026 — this reel is now true everywhere.** Four surfaces used to state the window as fact while this lesson called it a rule of thumb: `playTheTape` ("it always does, usually within 20 minutes"), the companion’s OFFLINE step ("Urges crest in 15–30 minutes and ALWAYS pass"), and both branches of the urge door. All four now carry the hedge, and a test holds it. So you can re-route the breath beats through the urge door without tapping through a sentence that contradicts you.

**SCREEN ACTIONS**
- 0–8s — Talking head, direct to camera.
- 8–14s — Screen: Fight tab, scroll down to the **"The toolkit"** card → **"Learn the tools — 2 min each"** → first row, **"Riding the wave"**.  ‹ On an account that has never tapped a practice button it is row 1 AND wears the gold **MAYBE START HERE** tag — so **shoot this beat BEFORE you rehearse 21–28s**, because tapping "Breathe one through now" marks this lesson picked up and moves the tag to another row. ›  Read the top: *"An urge is not a straight line that climbs until you give in. It rises, it peaks, and it comes down — whether or not you feed it."*
- 14–21s — Scroll to the evidence block and hold: *"The 'urges pass in 15–30 minutes' line you hear everywhere is a clinical rule of thumb, not a measured law — yours may be shorter or longer."*
- 21–28s — Tap **"Try the practice — one minute"**, then **"Breathe one through now."** **This launches SETTLE, not Reset** — the label reads **SETTLE**, the cues are **"Breathe in"** then **"Slow out"**, counting **"Round 1 of 8"**. There is no "Sip a little more" / "Long slow out" on this path. Let two full cycles play (about 20s), no talking over them.  ‹ **"Change breath" is on screen throughout the run** — you can pick Reset by name without leaving the Toolkit flow, if you want the cadence you originally wrote. ›
- 28–34s — **There is no 0–10 after-rating on this path — it is deliberately suppressed here.** It ends on *"Rest here a moment. That was a work — small, real, yours."* with a single button, **"I’m ready."** Tap it. Cut.  ‹ To film the 0–10 rating, shoot 21–34s from the urge door instead — and every word of your voiceover, CTA and caption stays true as written. ›

**VOICEOVER**
"The shape is real — up, peak, down, whether you feed it or not. That's the whole reason you can outlast one. The *number* is a rule of thumb from clinical practice, not something anyone measured. And that matters, because if you've been told twenty minutes and you're still standing there at twenty-five, you decide you're broken. You're not. Yours is just yours. So the app doesn't sell you a stopwatch — it gives you something to do with your body while it passes. Two of these. In, sip a bit more, long slow out. That's the fastest way down there is."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**
- "the SHAPE is real"
- "the NUMBER is a rule of thumb"
- "not a measured law"
- "if it's still there at 25 min — you're not the exception"

**THE TURN**
21s. The app hands you a mechanism instead of a countdown — and it corrected the myth the entire self-help internet repeats, including the version most quitting apps put on their home screen.

**CTA**
"Stop timing it. Next one, just do the breath — in, sip, long slow out — and stop checking the clock. Costs nothing, no app needed."

**CAPTION**
Correcting something I used to repeat: "urges last 15–20 minutes" is a clinical rule of thumb, not a finding. The shape is real — an urge rises, peaks and falls whether you feed it or not. The stopwatch isn't. And the difference matters, because when the timer runs out and it's still there, people decide they're the exception and give in. In · sip a little more · long slow out. Two of those.

## 5 · "DOES THIS WORK IF I'M NOT RELIGIOUS?"

## 12 · SET IT AT DAWN, LAY IT DOWN AT DUSK
**Format: day-in-the-life, two halves of one day**

**HOOK (0–2s)**
Spoken: *"This morning I wrote one sentence. Tonight the app read it back to me."*
On-screen text: **"6:40am"**
Visual: genuine morning. Kettle, curtains, bad hair. Do not tidy the room.

**THE PROBLEM**
You start the day meaning something, and by 9pm you couldn't tell anyone what it was.

**BEFORE THE TAKE (this script only):**
- **Shoot the night half BEFORE midnight.** Both evening cards are keyed to today’s DATE, not the hour — after 00:00 the morning’s sentence and the chips are gone and cannot be recovered. A "two halves of one day" reel has to be one real day.
- **The morning is ONE TAKE.** The tap that completes it locks it for the day, and the intention is read from the field **at that tap and never again** — a blank box at that instant means the evening has nothing to read back and the second half has no payoff.

Batch these — one screen-record session, one talking-head session, and two you have to catch in real life.

|---|---|---|

| **When you're ready, not before** | 2, 11 | 2 is the thesis video — worth being rested for. 11 is the scar one: only shoot it if a careless comment underneath wouldn't touch you. |

**Post #1 should be script 1.** Silent, no face, no voice, 34 seconds, and it shows the single most distinctive thing in the app. If it goes nowhere you've lost forty minutes and learned the format. If it lands, you have eleven more ready.

**Two rules from the playbook that apply hardest to these twelve:** every CTA above gives the mechanism away free so the video still helps someone who never installs anything — that's what stops a tutorial being an advert. And none of them claims a transformation, promises a timeline, or announces who you're becoming. They report what the thing does, in past tense, and let people draw their own conclusion.
