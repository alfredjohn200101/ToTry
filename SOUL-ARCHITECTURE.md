# To Try — The Soul Architecture
*Not a feature list. The intention behind every page, who it serves, and how a man moves through it.*

## The one sentence
To Try is the big brother a man never had — a presence that sees the WHOLE of him (money, mind,
body, spirit) and helps him wake with purpose, stay grounded in himself and in Christ, and keep
walking while the world pulls him toward his passions. Not a tracker. A brother.

## Who we build for
**One man, across his whole life.** He wakes wanting purpose. The day pulls at him from every side —
addictions, easy pleasures, an algorithm built to feed his passions, debt, doubt, exhaustion. He is
often the oldest, the one others lean on, with no one above him to say "I've walked this, here's
what's next, let me slap you back to reality with love." That's who this is. The man who needed a
big brother and never had one — so he becomes the proof that one can exist, even in a phone.

He is not a crisis. He is a whole life in motion. We serve him when he's winning AND when he's about
to fall — because a real brother is there for the whole climb, not just the cliff edge.

## The mechanism that makes it a brother, not a dashboard
Every other app is ONE feature of a man — a macro tracker, a workout log, a budget, a prayer app, a
sobriety counter. Each sees a sliver and is blind to the rest. To Try is the one home where they ALL
live together — and *because* they live together, the data stops being tracking and becomes COUNSEL.
A macro tracker logs the food. The brother knows he trained hard, slept badly, went over his line
last night, and his debt's been weighing on him — so he can say something TRUE that no single-feature
app could ever say. The integration is not a feature. It is the entire point.

Every screen must do more than track. It must answer at least one of:
**what's next for him · what's wrong · what can he do better.** If it only reports, it isn't done.

## The line we will not cross
Feeding passions for engagement is what harms men. This app must be the OPPOSITE — it pulls a man
back to himself and to Christ, even at the cost of its own screen time. It points beyond itself: to
the sacraments, to real relationships, to prayer. It never makes itself the authority (Magnifica
Humanitas — AI as signpost, not god). A brother who keeps you for himself is not a brother. This one
gives you back to your life, better than it found you.

## THE FIVE TABS (the spine the thumb knows)

*from* these, never floating loose. A man should never wonder "where am I."

## EVERY PAGE — purpose · who it serves · the feeling · how it connects

### TODAY (home) — *the front door*
- **Purpose:** orient the man the second he opens the app. Tell him where he is in his day and the
  ONE thing that matters now.
- **Serves:** both. The 11pm man (the companion is one reach away) and the winning man (his days-in,
  his becoming, right at the top).
- **Feeling:** "this knows me, this is with me." The aurora breathes with the time of day so the app
  is a companion through the day, not a static board.
- **Connects:** → morning/evening ritual (the action button), → companion (the breathing orb,
  reachable from everywhere), → everything else below the fold, quieter.
- **DONE (v159):** the breathing front-door hero. The rest of the home below it still needs to be
  ordered by *meaning* (what serves the man now), not by accretion.

### THE COMPANION (not a tab — a presence) — *the heart*
- **Purpose:** the 11pm moment. Meet the man in the exact second of temptation.
- **Serves:** the 11pm man. This is the whole reason the app exists.
- **Feeling:** a friend who already knows your day, doesn't flinch, doesn't preach. Reachable from
  ANY screen with one thumb-motion (the breathing orb → should become a swipe-up sheet).
- **Connects:** knows everything (sleep, training, vices, faith) so it can say "the pull is strong
  because you're exhausted, not weak." Wins flow into the record (streak, milestones).
- **DONE (v534):** it sizes to its content and rises from the bottom. Asking one short question it
  stands 549px on a 414x896 phone with 347px of the app still visible behind it; a long conversation
  still gets every pixel it had before (832px), so no helpline can be pushed off-screen. Capped at
  exactly the height it used to be fixed at — it can only ever be shorter now, never taller.

### MORNING — *set the day*
- **Purpose:** before the day starts, choose who you'll be in it. Intention as armor.
- **Serves:** the winning man, building the day on purpose. (And protects the 11pm man preemptively.)
- **Feeling:** quiet, unhurried, sacred. A dawn-lit moment, not a checklist.
- **Connects:** sleep rating → readiness (feeds companion + coach all day). Intention → referenced
  by the companion if the pull comes later ("you said this morning you wanted...").
- **DONE (v536):** a sunrise sits behind the tab, and the ritual is five steps — Arrive, Receive,
  Your body, Set the day, Close — each fitting one screen, instead of 1913px of scroll and 42
  controls. Every card and handler is unchanged; blocks are assigned to steps by anchor, so anything
  added later inherits the step it sits in rather than disappearing. "Show the whole morning" is
  always one tap away for anyone who wants it all at once.

### EVENING (reflect) — *close the day with grace*
- **Purpose:** look back honestly, without shame. End the day reconciled.
- **Serves:** both. The winning man sees his evidence; the struggling man receives grace for a hard day.
- **Feeling:** dusk, examen, peace. "You showed up. Tomorrow is new."
- **Connects:** the examen feeds the weekly reflection + the record. Watch rings, today's wins.
- **DONE (v537):** a dusk gradient behind the tab — the light going, where the morning'''s is coming —
  and seven steps instead of 27 blocks in one scroll: Land, One win, Release, What you did, Tomorrow,
  Reflection, Close. Grace-first is the ordering, not a tone: the day'''s evidence and a win land
  before anything is released, and the three good things come before the examen, which sits last with
  the completion. "Show the whole evening" is one tap away.

### FIGHT — *the war room, but tender*
- **Purpose:** the standing record of the battles — vices, streaks, recovery, the live moderation counter.
- **Serves:** both. The 11pm man (recovery timeline = "here's what your streak is earning you"),
  the winning man (his clean days made visible and shareable).
- **Feeling:** not military, not shaming. Strong AND tender. Grace after relapse lives here.
- **Connects:** ← companion wins land here. → recovery timeline, → money reclaimed, → share cards.
- **DONE (v535):** the clean clock says how long; directly beneath it now sits what has been WON —
  "3 urges met and turned away this week · £46 reclaimed · your longest run yet — past 21 days" —
  before the per-vice list, which used to open with "WIN RATE 0%". Counted from the fight log, the
  vice→money pipe and the real slip history; silent when none of it is earned.

### GROW (hub) → TRAIN · NOURISH · TRACK — *the body as one loop*
- **Purpose:** the body honestly. Train → fuel you've earned → the result you can see.
- **Serves:** the winning man, building. The doctor's cause→effect made visible.
- **Feeling:** one connected loop, not three tabs. "You trained, so you're owed this fuel; here's
  what your body did with it."
- **Connects:** train burns → nourish's net → track's trend. Sleep → readiness → train's "today's a
  walk not a PR." Strava/Hevy feed it. The honest equation ties it together.
- **DONE (v533):** reordered to Train→Nourish→Track, and each card now hands off to the next with a
  live line — "3 sessions this week, about 1,260 cal earned → fuel it" / "5 of 7 days fuelled, 52g
  protein a day → see what it did" / "-0.8kg over the week — that is what the training and the fuel
  did." Counted from what was actually logged, never estimated; silent when there is nothing true to
  say, and when someone has trained but logged no food it names that broken link instead of a stat.

### SOUL (hub) → BIBLE · LITURGY · WHY · (prayer) — *the root*
- **Purpose:** the faith that the whole app is built on (Magnifica Humanitas — AI as signpost, not
  authority; the sacraments and real relationships above the app itself).
- **Serves:** both, at the root. The reason "the least one can do is try" means anything.
- **Feeling:** reverent, rooted, never preachy. The app points beyond itself here.
- **Connects:** the verse on home comes from here. The "why" is referenced by the companion + coach.
  Liturgy roots the day in something bigger than the self.
- **DONE (v538):** it opens with the still centre — one word for today from the person'''s own
  tradition and one thing to do with it (be still for a minute) — above the four grids of features.
  A secular person is offered the stillness and no scripture at all: "One minute of stillness.
  Nothing to believe, nothing to say — just your breath, and the room."

### MONEY — *stewardship, not spreadsheets*
- **Purpose:** money as discipline and stewardship — including the vice→reclaimed pipe (money not
  spent on the vice becomes visible freedom).
- **Serves:** the winning man, and the 11pm man (every clean day = real money reclaimed = another
  reason the streak matters).
- **Feeling:** calm command, not anxiety. Faith-rooted stewardship.
- **Connects:** ← vice costs from Fight become reclaimed totals here. Debt-free date, savings.
- **DONE (v539):** the reclaimed figure now sits ABOVE the Paid off / Debt left metrics once there is
  one — $143 reclaimed by staying clean before the $14,000 still owed. When it is still zero the debt
  table leads, because a "$0 reclaimed" hero at the top of the screen would be worse than the figures.

### COACH — *the omniscient guide*
- **Purpose:** ask anything; get an answer that knows your whole life.
- **Serves:** both. Reachable, knows body+soul+fight+money+sleep.
- **Feeling:** a wise guide who's read your whole story. Signpost, not authority (per the encyclical).
- **Connects:** reads getLifeState (everything). The weekly question routes here.

### CALENDAR — *the week with meaning*
- **Purpose:** the rhythm of the days, not just appointments.
- **Serves:** the winning man, building rhythm. Wake/bed times drive the day's flow.
- **TODO:** earn its place or fold into Today. Question first.

### SETTINGS — *quiet utility*
- **Purpose:** the controls. Doesn't need soul-theater, needs clarity and calm.
- **Serves:** both, invisibly. Identity ("who I'm becoming") is set here — that one matters.

## THE DESIGN LANGUAGE (one soul, applied everywhere)
- **Daypart aurora** — dawn/day/dusk/night skins, so the whole app breathes with time.
- **Three-layer elevation** — base/raised/overlay, consistent depth (DONE, v158).
- **Spring motion that confirms, never performs** — every tap answers "did that land?"
- **Bottom-sheets** for secondary content (the iOS convention thumbs expect).
- **One focus per screen** — earn attention before asking for it; never a wall of cards.
- **Cormorant serif for the soul-voice; DM Mono for the quiet data-voice.** Already the palette.
- **Grace over shame, always. Presence over measurement. The app points beyond itself.**

## THE BUILD ORDER (intention, not vanity)
1. Companion as a bottom-sheet (the heart — most important thing in the app).
2. Morning + Evening rebuilt to FEEL like their time of day (the daily rhythm).
3. The body loop (Train→Nourish→Track) made to flow as one.
4. Fight reframed as "your fight, and how you're winning."
5. Soul as the still center.
6. Money as stewardship.
7. Home below-the-fold ordered by meaning.
8. Calendar: question its existence; fold if it doesn't serve.
9. Cohesion pass: every screen inherits the daypart + elevation + one-focus language.
