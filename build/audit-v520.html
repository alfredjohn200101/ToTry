NOURISH — food, nutrition, fuel plan

The Nourish tab is a full calorie/macro/micro food diary with day navigation (±120 days), five logging doors (typeahead search over four online DBs plus 77 curated offline staples, barcode scan, AI meal photo, voice, quick add), a per-food correction store, recipes, saved meals and repeat-a-day. On top of the diary sits a coaching layer the mainstream trackers don't have: a nourishment quality score, a "why am I eating" hunger check, adaptive TDEE from real intake vs weight trend, a weekly digest, calorie/carb cycling, water and fasting. Section 3 is the AI "fuel plan" — a day of real meals plus a store-split priced shopping list, timed to the person's actual gym schedule. Its stated intent is counsel rather than counting, with a gentle "numbers off" mode as the deliberate anti-eating-disorder antidote.

What it takes from the competition, and how

  • MyFitnessPal — tap-the-row-for-portion / (+) to log instantly (_makeFoodResultRow 14-nourish.js:484), meal-grouped diary with a per-meal calorie total and '+ Add food' under each meal (renderNutritionLog 2894), the Base goal − Food = Remaining equation strip (shell-head.html:2361-2371), calorie-by-meal split (renderMealSplit 3302), and the CSV migration card with export instructions (renderMigrateCard 3219). Reproduced completely on the logging side, and improved on: exercise is shown as already inside the target (eq-burn, 14-nourish.js:3082) instead of MFP's eat-it-back double count.
  • MacroFactor — adaptive TDEE from real intake vs trend weight (computeAdaptiveTDEE 3343). Implemented more carefully than a copy: least-squares regression over every weigh-in in the window rather than an endpoint difference, plus a coverage guard (≥5 days AND ≥60% of the window) that MacroFactor doesn't need because it nags you to log. Its macro discipline is also reproduced honestly in macrosForCalories (3479): protein floor by bodyweight, fat minimum for hormones, carbs absorb the rest. The one-tap target update is consent-based (setCalGoalFromTDEE 3667), never automatic.
  • Cronometer — the 24-nutrient micro panel (renderMicroPanel 3953, MICRO_KEYS in addFoodToLog 2170) with correct per-100g scaling. Partial by data rather than by code: micros only exist when the source supplied them, and the nourishment score deliberately treats them as a footnote rather than a penalty so a text-logged whole-food meal isn't punished.
  • Cal AI — photo-to-macros (handleMealPhoto 832). Deliberately different, not just visually: the prompt asks for each plate component separately, and _renderPhotoMeal (885) makes every item individually scalable (_pmAdj), editable (_pmEdit), removable (_pmRemove), plus a one-tap 'hidden cooking fat' add (_pmAddFat) and a save-for-identical-relogging (_pmSave). Cal AI returns one blob and charges for it.
  • LoseIt/MFP retention — the logging streak (computeNutStreak 2531, celebrateNutMilestone 2540). Reproduced but softened: it appears as a small line next to the date, never as a loss-framed push.
  • MFP/Lifesum barcode scanning — reproduced and extended: native BarcodeDetector fast path, AI-vision fallback, and a manual number entry, plus the thing no mainstream tracker offers — a permanent per-barcode correction (saveFoodOverride 1956) instead of scrolling duplicate community entries.

What is missing

  • No crisis or eating-disorder language gate on the Nourish free-text doors. detectCrisis() is called at 22 sites across the app; zero of them are in 14-nourish.js or 17-fuelplan.js. The main search box ('Search food, describe a meal…') routes any multi-word text to estimateMealMacros()/searchFoodOnline() ungated. The fuel plan's 'Allergies, foods you hate, anything else…' field does have a check, but it is a bespoke narrower regex (_fuelElicitSave, 17-fuelplan.js:114) rather than the app's own detector — two mechanisms for one job, and the weaker one guards the food surface.
  • exportFoodCSV (app.js:3582) writes only Date, Meal, Food, Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat. It drops the serving label, the qty and all 24 micronutrients the app records — so 'Export my food log' cannot round-trip the app's own data even before the date bug below.
  • No way to set or edit the water goal from inside Nourish; waterGoalMl (4239) reads totry_water_goal, which is only writable from Settings (28-init.js:540). The card shows '0 / 2.5 L' with no route to change it.
  • The weekly digest still resolves a day from entries[0].ts only (renderNutWeeklyDigest 3251). computeAdaptiveTDEE was hardened with dayTsFromKey (3358) to parse the en-AU key when a ts is missing; the digest was not given the same fallback, so the two 7-day readers can disagree about which days exist.
  • No per-recipe serving count. openRecipeBuilder/saveCurrentRecipe (1575/1749) sum ingredients into one total and logRecipeAsMeal (1777) logs the whole thing — a recipe that feeds four cannot be logged as one portion.

TRAIN + TRACK — workouts, routines, body, readiness, sleep
