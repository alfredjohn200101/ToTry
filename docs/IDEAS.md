# The idea list — 26 generated, 18 judged, what survived and what convergence says

> *"more things we can do with totry that others are yet to do in this regard that is beneficial for
> self improvement and would never otherwise see in a free app."*

Six lenses, blind to each other — **anti-app**, **longitudinal**, **moment**, **professional**,
**threads**, **underserved**. Each produced 4–5 ideas; my harness took only the top 3 per lens to
refutation, so **nine were never judged at all**. That cap is recorded here rather than hidden,
because two of the eight are among the strongest on the page.

Every judged idea met refuters told to **default to refuted**. One survived. That is the harness
working — but a kill from a hostile refuter is evidence about *the argument as written*, not proof
the idea is worthless, which is why the killed ones are printed in full below.

## Killed — 16

### anti-app
- **The Handover** *(medium)* — when the app can see exactly which hour you are in danger, it tries to put a real person in that hour instead of itself, then stops buzzing you, because someone better is on it.
- **The Note You Take With You** *(large)* — one page written for the actual human you are about to sit in front of, so the eight minutes you get with them are not spent trying to remember.
- **The Redundancy Audit** *(small)* — every reminder gets asked, out loud, whether it is still doing any work; the ones that are not get switched off, with the evidence shown.

### longitudinal
- **What Left Without You Noticing** *(small)* — a quiet census, once a season, of practices that were genuinely part of your life and have silently stopped, with three answers, one of which is to let it go honestly and never hear about it again.
- **The Ignition** *(medium)* — watches for the days your good stretches **began** (not what they contained) and tells you what happened in the week before each one — nearly always on a completely different front from the stretch itself.

### moment
- **The Stacked Day** *(medium)* — on the two or three days a month when your whole life is stacked against you it comes earlier and softer, and on every ordinary day it says nothing at all.
- **The Hour After** *(small)* — ninety minutes after a slip, because the damage was never the slip; it is the *"I have broken it anyway"* that arrives while nobody is looking.
- **What Actually Works On You** *(medium)* — stops handing you the next technique in the list and starts handing you the one you have actually come back from.

### professional
- **Your Rule (a rule of life)** *(medium)* — once a season you write six short lines about the life you actually want, across body, prayer, people, money and rest; the app never asks you about them again because it already knows, and at the season's end shows which four became how you live.
- **The Handover — the page you take to a human** *(medium)* — one page of your own organised evidence (sleep, mood, weight, drinking, cycle, or income and outgoings) for a GP, a counsellor or a free debt adviser.
- **The fast that knows your body and your tradition** *(small)* — through Ramadan, Lent, Navratri, Uposatha or a fast a person sets themselves, it places protein, water, training and sleep around the real times of that day, and says honestly when their own tradition says they are not obliged.

### threads
- **Outgrown** *(small)* — switches its own features off once you have visibly stopped needing them, tells you it did, and keeps a list of what it handed back.
- **Protein on what's left** *(medium)* — the week's meal plan built to the money actually in the grocery budget, naming the cheapest real protein on your own list.

### underserved
- **The Wake-Anchored Day** *(large)* — stops assuming your day starts in the morning; learns when you actually wake and anchors everything to that, so a night nurse's 7am dinner is dinner.
- **What's In The House** *(medium)* — for someone with no money left, the only question that matters: what can I actually eat between now and payday, out of what is already in the kitchen plus what little is left — and honestly when the answer is *"this isn't enough, here is real help."*
- **Given Days** *(small)* — when someone comes back after a stretch away, it asks once what the days **were**, and if they went on someone else, on a loss, or on being ill, it re-reads its own history so those days count as **given**, not missed.

## What the list says that no single idea does

**Three lenses, blind to each other, produced the same idea: a page you hand to a real human.**
"Something To Take With You", "The Note You Take With You" and "The Handover — the page you take to
a human" are one feature described three times. Two of the three were killed and one survived, which
is a fact about how each argument was written, not about the idea. Convergence across independent
generators is stronger evidence than any single refuter's verdict.

**Two lenses produced the same fast.** "The fast that knows your body and your tradition" and "The
fast is a body too" are the same feature. The app already has the `FAITHS` registry and already
holds the body; the gap is that the nutrition side currently *argues* with a fast instead of serving
it.

Both point at the two things this app can do that nothing free does:
1. **It already knows your tradition** — so a fast, a season and a rule of life are native here and
   nowhere else.
2. **It already refuses to be the endpoint** — so "here is the page, go and see a human" is the one
   feature an engagement-funded app structurally cannot ship.

## The recurring reasons things were killed

Worth reading before building any of them, because every one of these is a real constraint:

1. **It needs data nobody has yet.** Most of the longitudinal and moment ideas need months of
   history. `THE-GAP.md` carries the same caveat about `fromPlan`.
2. **It is a judgement wearing a measurement's clothes.** "What actually works on you" needs a
   causal claim the data cannot support — the person who came back may have come back anyway.
3. **It can shame.** "You have stopped praying" is true, cheap to compute, and against the soul of
   the app.
4. **It duplicates something already built.** Several were versions of surfaces that exist.

## The nine, judged — 30 Sep 2026

Judged after the fact, on the owner's instruction, because the cap that dropped them was mine and
some of them clearly made sense. Four lenses per idea, each told to default to REFUTED —
**already-built?** (grep the constants and DRIVE it; this repo's most repeated false finding is "X is
missing" when X exists), **data reality** (which `totry_*` stores, are they actually WRITTEN, and how
long before it can say anything true), **soul** (write the exact sentence it says on the person's
WORST week), and **harm** (what does it do to someone mid-relapse, mid-urge, newly bereaved).

**Every one came back BUILD WITH CORRECTION. Not one was killed and not one survived unchanged** —
which, after the first round where 17 of 18 were killed outright, is the difference between refuting
a pitch and examining an idea against the actual code. Five are marked *only we could do this*: they
need the whole person in one place.

**The judging was worth more for what it found by accident.** Three lenses, chasing three unrelated
ideas, each tripped over ungated free-text doors. That thread — 13 doors, 22 → 35 — is the most
important thing in this document and has nothing to do with any of these features.

### You've Been Here Before — `BUILD WITH CORRECTION`  ·  **only we could do this**
> The retrieval half is real and nobody else can do it; the "that got you out" half is a causal claim resting on a field the app has never written — close that one-line hole, drop the verdict from the title, and it becomes the best counsel this app can give.

**Corrected:** Build "What You Left Yourself": record the exit a person chose at each `_feelMove` row (six call sites, one field — this fills the `moved` field that today exists only in the demo seeder at 27-settings.js:486) plus the breath's own 0–10 post-scale, then MATCH IN THE CALM and DELIVER ONE MOVE IN THE MOMENT — "you left yourself something for this," their own logged act, no date, no count, no comparison, and silence (plus a door to a human) when the history is thin or every past instance of that shape ended badly. Ship the honest slice today with zero new fields: `totry_breath_log` already carries reason + before/after, so "last time the pull hit you at an 8, the sigh took you to a 4" is live at three rated sessions (`_breathProof`, 06-train.js:70).

**The objection that survived:** The second half of the sentence has no data behind it. Of the seven exits `_feelMove()` offers, exactly one — guided breathing — carries `{reason: feeling}` through to a store with a before/after pair. Train, journal, companion, look up, natural highs and smallest-thing leave no trace linking back to the feeling that sent the person there, `totry_moments_won`/`totry_feeling_wins` record THAT a moment was won and never how, and the field literally named for this (`totry_feeling_wins.moved`) lives only in the demo seeder while both real writers omit it and no reader reads it. Read the seed, the feature looks supported; read the writers, it isn't — this repo's own most-repeated trap. Second objection, structurally worse: the corpus can only contain days the person LOGGED, so the search space is systematically their FUNCTIONING past, and on a fifth day-one the nearest neighbour to the shape IS the last day-one — the title becomes a citation for the loop the person is most afraid of.

**Already there:** Partially, and all of it one-dimensional. `feelingPattern()`/`feelingCount()` (05-moment.js:115/131) drive the door's `_recur` block ("the 4th time restless has brought you here this week") — but the key is one of 10 enum ids, the window is hard-coded to 7 days, and `_savedBanner` replays the plan the person WROTE (`totry_if_then`), not what they DID. `analyzeUrgePatterns()` (04-fight.js:1427) and `renderTriggerPatternCard()` (09-fight-deep.js:1126) mine `totry_fight_log` for aggregate hour/weekday/trigger patterns, and `renderHomeInsight()` (32-presence.js:290) does 28-day cohort correlations — group-level, never "that day, and what you did next". `walkBackLinks()` and Train's "Last time: 100kg × 5" are per-entity, not per-state. Nothing in the tree does state-similarity retrieval: greps for similar/nearest/closest/distance/cosine/vector/snapshot across src/app return zero, and none of the ~260 `totry_*` keys holds a per-day state series. `totry_mood_log` is in SYNC_KEYS and the array-merge list with no write site anywhere — a dead key.

*Cost: large. Useful after: The breath slice is useful immediately (3 rated sessions, ~2 weeks of real use, no new fields). The recorded-exit half starts speaking ~4–6 weeks after the one-line write ships. The full multi-front shape match needs a historical-state reconstructor first — `getLifeState()` is hard-anchored to `Date.now()` and there is no per-day state series — so months.*

### The Season, Closed — `BUILD WITH CORRECTION`  ·  **only we could do this**
> The premise is exactly true and the defect underneath it is worse than the feature — but build the record and the ASK, not the closing ceremony.

**Corrected:** Give the season a start, an end and an append-only `totry_seasons` history ({name, startTs, endTs}, union-by-id in the ARR registry at src/app/01-sync.js:481, never the scalar rule; boundaries via dayKeyOf()), and then make the app ASK — once, in the evening, and at restartJourney() — "you named this a Building season in March; is that still where you are?" If a close renders at all it is a NAMING, not a counting: the person's own line plus the one number that cannot shame ("Grieving — 174 days. You carried that, and you kept opening this"), suppressed entirely for Grieving and Resting unless they open it, and never auto-fired on the transition tap.

**The objection that survived:** The closing fires at exactly the wrong moment. People change Building→Fighting because they relapsed last night and Celebrating→Grieving because someone died this morning — so "here is what that chapter contained" answers an honest admission of a fall with an inventory of what was lost. The app already decided this the other way: restartJourney() (src/app/03-person.js:2396) is a chapter close that deliberately shows nothing about what ended ("You fell, you're here, you're going again"), and two chapter systems telling opposite stories about the same months is worse than one. Worse, a new reader written to the brief "show what it actually contained" reads ls('totry_journal') raw — nine existing readers filter `flagged`, and the tenth would hand a bereaved woman her own 3am suicide disclosure back, captioned as the summary of her mother's death. And two of the six seasons are DEFINED as having nothing to show (Grieving: "Survival mode is okay"; Resting: "Not pushing"), against explicit no-clock doctrine at 08-voice.js:133 and 09-fight-deep.js:740.

**Already there:** The summariser is built and the stores are all dated — computeYearInReview() (src/app/24-review.js:375) already aggregates eighteen whole-life fields through one predicate, `inYear(ts)`, and the year is the only year-specific thing in it; extract computeRange(startTs,endTs) rather than writing a second copy. showSynthesisHistory() (:566) is the pattern for a list of past chapters. What does NOT exist: any season start, end, or history — `totry_season` is a bare scalar written in exactly two real places (onboarding src/app/03-person.js:2865, the picker src/app/40-identity-seasons.js:159), and grep for totry_seasons/seasonLog/seasonStart across src, scripts and test returns zero. Three caveats for the reuse: viceSavings (delegates to totalReclaimed(), lifetime), longestStreak (walks all relapseHistory) and savedVerses/prCount are NOT windowed — harmless over a year, visibly wrong over six weeks.

*Cost: medium. Useful after: The ask lands in weeks (first evening prompt after ~30 days); the record is useful at the person's second season, which for most people is the first time they ever revisit that word. There is no computation here that needs months — every figure is a count over a self-sizing window.*

### When I'm The Scroll — `BUILD WITH CORRECTION`  ·  **only we could do this**
> The detection is right and the app's own thesis cashed in; the destination is wrong — four opens in twenty minutes with nothing written is the urge signature, not the scroll signature, so it must open the Feeling Door, not the exit.

**Corrected:** Keep the detector — a device-local ring buffer of the last ~6 `visibilitychange`→visible timestamps (NOT `initApp`, which never re-runs on a Capacitor resume; NOT `totry_last_open_ts`, which is overwritten and written on a 4s delay; and NOT in SYNC_KEYS) plus an in-memory "did something" flag set by real write sites rather than by `go()` — but invert the destination: on four bounces with nothing written the app says nothing about the person and simply opens the existing Feeling Door, with ONE new chip ("Nothing — just picked the phone up") as the only path to `theRelease()`. And it must not fire at all inside `fight.hardHour`, within 72h of a slip, during an active rest timer / fast / in-progress meal log, after any `detectCrisis` hit this session, for a guest, or in a person's first week — and it must never say the number, and never call `_logRelease()` unless the person chose that chip and took the handoff.

**The objection that survived:** The trigger is a false positive for exactly the person the app exists for, and the app's own clinical copy proves it. `src/app/05-moment.js:1760` tells a person mid-urge to "Set fifteen minutes and decide at the end of it"; `04-fight.js:845` puts the porn wave at 5-15 minutes, `:876` puts nicotine at 3-5. A man who does precisely what the app taught him — sets the delay, puts the phone down, picks it up to check the timer, opens Fight, reads the science line, opens again — produces four opens, twenty minutes, zero writes. At minute eighteen of a wave the app would tell him it is not what he is looking for and offer to leave, and he would accept, because that is what anyone mid-urge does with permission to stop trying. ToTry has no feed, so the true-positive population for "using me as the scroll" is nearly empty while the false-positive population is the hard hour. Second: `theRelease()` calls `_logRelease()` unconditionally (05-moment.js:474), so shipping it as written would inflate `totry_releases` — the one number the app is proud of, shown on Home as "N times you came here and walked back into your life" — with people who left worse than they arrived.

**Already there:** The exit half is fully built and is one of the best things in the app: `theRelease(opts)` (src/app/05-moment.js:468) with `_logRelease`/`releaseCount` (:466-467), `totry_releases`, `nextSmallThingHTML()` embedded, 26 call sites — every one of which fires after a COMPLETED action and names what the person just did. The Feeling Door (`FEELINGS`, 05-moment.js:3-14, ten chips) and `_feelMove('restless')` (:212, "That buzz wants OUT, not in... 20 push-ups right now, or a fast walk") are the correct destination and already exist. `totry_nudge_dismissed` (32-presence.js:257) gives the 20h dismissal for free. The DETECTOR does not exist in any form: `checkReturnNudge()` (02-native.js:993-1023) handles the opposite case (>20h away) and destroys the previous timestamp on every launch (:1007); `logEvent('open')` is remote-only and off under `metricsOff()`; nothing counts opens, sessions, or whether a screen produced an action. It is on the roadmap only — docs/IDEAS.md:34 and :173 (ranked 3rd), and docs/SCREEN-TIME.md names the app's own session time as "Buildable now, zero permission... The only number that can tell a tool from a feed." Do NOT derive "did they act" from `totry_key_ts`: Health.autoSync writes `totry_today_steps` and `totry_trackers` on every resume (02-native.js:209, :414), so it would answer "yes, they acted" most reliably for the most engaged people — firing the accusation backwards.

*Cost: medium. Useful after: ~20 minutes from install. This is the only idea in the batch whose entire input is twenty minutes old — no history, no streak, no baseline, no backfill, and the `visibilitychange` event it needs is already wired and already firing (`src/app/02-native.js:416`). Most people will never trip it, and the ones who do are exactly who it is for. Silence is the complete and correct empty state; there is no empty-state copy to write.*

### The Handing Back — `BUILD WITH CORRECTION`
> The gap it names is real and nobody else has it — disclosure in this app only ever runs one way, the app can get louder and never quieter — but the detection signal must be deleted: silence has no signature, so build the quieting, refuse the inference and refuse the exit.

**Corrected:** Make disclosure TWO-WAY AND SILENT: let the existing `gates` in `applyHomeProgressiveDisclosure()` (31-nextstep.js:195) re-close on a front with no writes in N days, with zero copy, fully reversible, auto-unhiding the moment that front has content again and one always-present "Show me what I put away" in Settings — the person notices only that Home got calmer, and the app never makes a claim about them. Then add the loud half as a PERSON-DECLARED action beside the Season picker (40-identity-seasons.js:127) — "this one's behind me" — which hands them what the front cost and what it bought and names the real people who carried them, never offered by the app, never for the Fight, never for any `kind==='letgo'` card, never for Soul, never for anything on the crisis path, and never replaying a stored free-text string.

**The objection that survived:** Silence has no signature, and this codebase has already ruled on it twice in its own comments. Three people with an identical 90-day-quiet Fight — (a) genuinely free, (b) relapsed on day four and stopped opening the tab because they could not face the counter, (c) stopped logging food because logging was the harm (`nutGentle()` exists for exactly them) — are byte-identical in storage. `09-fight-deep.js:2161` says it outright: a count of elapsed silence "flatters exactly the person who most needs the truth." The Handing Back proposes to build that inference as a feature and then act on it destructively: the offer routes to `removeVice()`, which warns "Your fight history for it will be lost" and writes `_tombAdd('totry_v', name)` so the cloud merge can never bring it back. And the card it would delete is the only door to `openLettingGo()` and `_letGoWentBack` ("Reaching back is part of letting go... Nothing resets"). The asymmetry is the whole argument: a false "you still need this" costs a person nothing; a false "you don't" closes the door at 11pm on the one night it mattered.

**Already there:** The folding machinery exists in four forms and the withdrawal SHAPE exists once, correctly. `applyHomeProgressiveDisclosure()` (31-nextstep.js:195) gates eight home cards on `daysInstalled()` and is strictly one-way — there is no inverse branch anywhere in the bundle. `renderHomeDepthFold()`/`toggleHomeDepth()` (31-nextstep.js:227) and `applyTabIntroDisclosure()` (31-nextstep.js:180) both fold reversibly, but on presence of data, never absence of use. The reach-out stand-down (`_reachUnansweredRun()` 02-native.js:610, `_resolveReachOuts()` :668) is exactly the idea's posture including the say-it-plainly part — "standing down — the last few went unanswered, so I've stopped spending your attention" — but it stands down the VOICE, never a surface. What does not exist: any per-front usage or dormancy signal (`daysQuiet` is one `Math.max` over four stores only — workouts, evenings, checkins, journal), an inverse gate, tab removal (`TABS` is a fixed literal and the nav highlight is positional, `TABS.indexOf` → `querySelectorAll('.nb')[i]`), or any per-front exit. Two independent bugs surfaced underneath this that are worth more than the feature: `addVice()` (09-fight-deep.js:1319) and `createLetGo()` (05-moment.js:433) both store free text with NO crisis gate while `obPersistVices()` gates the identical onboarding field — these are crisis doors 21 and 22 and should ship this week; and `daysQuiet` ignores nutlog, water, trackers, transactions, prayers, fight_log, feelings and breath, so a person whose whole life is in the food diary already reads as "gone quiet" to the AI brief.

*Cost: medium. Useful after: The person-declared handing-back works on day one, for everybody, including every existing user. The silent re-fold works from roughly day 14–30 and needs no new usage log at all — every store in the app evicts oldest-first (`_capWorkouts`, `_pruneNutLog`, `unshift`+`slice`), so a `lastWroteTo(front)` over stores that already carry `ts` is ~20 lines and its recency never decays. The version that needs a per-screen visit log is the version to drop: `go()` (03-person.js:5171) persists nothing, and a log started today answers nothing for anyone who already has the app.*

### The Two Kinds of Opening — `BUILD WITH CORRECTION`
> The trigger is right and the escalation is already written and dead in the code — but the "two kinds" ratio and the rising-number framing must be thrown away: fire on an absolute floor of trouble-opens, show no number, and call bridgeToRealHelp('persistent') once.

**Corrected:** When a person has come to the app in distress at or above a fixed floor across a fixed window (derived from the nine non-'good' chips in `totry_feelings`, crisis-flagged rows excluded), offer once — on the next CALM open or in the evening, never mid-moment — `bridgeToRealHelp('persistent')` (src/app/04-fight.js:1654, zero callers today) with a named person from `totry_your_few`, then hold for 30 days. No count is ever rendered, no trend is ever computed, nothing enters `lifeStateBrief()` as a figure, and a falling count changes nothing — the number going down is what someone who stopped reaching looks like.

**The objection that survived:** The trend inverts the app's own thesis. A rising count of opening-in-trouble is the behaviour this product exists to produce — reaching for help instead of the thing that hurts — and the app already says so twice on the home screen (`releaseCount()` at 05-moment.js:477 and 31-nextstep.js:313, and "honour it" in the coach prompt at 03-person.js:1349). Printing the same number as a warning puts two verdicts on one number in one app, and hands the person a cheap way to make it fall: don't tap the orb at 2am. Grief, a luteal week, Lent or a redundancy all triple that count without anything getting worse — and once it falls, the feature stands down exactly when they went quiet. Secondary: the "use it" denominator genuinely does not exist (the three `totry_last_open*` keys are boot-only scalars, and `totry_usage_log` counts AI tokens), so the ratio as worded is unbuildable anyway — which is fine, because the corrected version needs no denominator.

**Already there:** Almost all of the machinery. `totry_feelings` ({id,ts}, single writer at 05-moment.js:139, in SYNC_KEYS and the union list so the count is a real cross-device count) already holds the trouble stream, and FEELINGS at 05-moment.js:3-14 already carries the nine-trouble/one-'good' taxonomy. `feelingCount(id,days)` and `feelingPattern(days)` exist. The app ALREADY escalates on this number at 05-moment.js:246-257 ("this is the Nth time <feeling> has brought you here this week") — but routes to an if-then plan, never a human. And the destination is written and dead: `bridgeToRealHelp('persistent')` at 04-fight.js:1654 says verbatim the sentence this idea wants to earn, and a grep of all 42 modules finds no caller — every live site passes 'heavy'. What is genuinely missing is one reader over the trouble class as a whole, one call site, and one hold. The opposite reading is also already shipped and must be left alone: `releaseCount()` (05-moment.js:466-467, shown at 05-moment.js:477 and 31-nextstep.js:313-317).

*Cost: small. Useful after: Two to three weeks of the person's own use before the floor can honestly be reached — and it must say nothing at all until then, which is correct behaviour, not a gap. Build cost is roughly a day: one reader, one call site on a calm open or the evening, the 30-day hold via the existing `_setReachState({pausedUntil})`, plus seeding `totry_feelings` in scripts/seed-person.mjs (it is seeded nowhere today, so the whole gate is blind to this store — CLAUDE.md lesson (l)) and a fault-injected test that plants a 'good' tap and a crisis-flagged row and requires neither to be counted.*

### Hand It Down — `BUILD WITH CORRECTION`
> The instinct is the founder's own thesis turned outward and it is the one idea whose success happens off the phone — but the app must never be the thing that declares a person "come through"; delete the trigger, let the person open the door, and inherit the crisis filters.

**Corrected:** Hand It Down is a door the person opens — "someone I know is where I was" — never a card the app fires: it pulls their own passages (through safeJournal()-shaped readers, flagged rows dropped, never raw stores) from the season they choose, and ends in one passage, one named person from totry_relationships, and three honest outs — send it, read it to them, or keep it and tell them in your own words. Build it beside totry_letters (saveLetterFromWhy, src/app/25-soul.js:442) as the same sealed-words mechanism with the recipient changed; no counter, no log, no streak, no share card, no model rewriting their words, no auto-picked recipient. Precondition, and fix it today whatever happens to this idea: saveLetterFromWhy() calls detectCrisis() NOWHERE (verified — test/crisis.js has 19 doors and none is the letter box), so "Dear future me…" takes a suicide disclosure ungated and deliverLetter() (src/app/24-review.js:758) replays it verbatim with haptic('celebrate') up to 730 days later. Add that door, plus a door for the new compose field and one for the description of who you are handing it to.

**The objection that survived:** "When you have genuinely come through something" is a verdict the app cannot make and must not make. Clean days are abstinence, not recovery, and 94 days sits inside the window where relapse is most likely. ## BUILT, 30 Sep 2026 — and it was a defect, not a feature

I ranked the fast first and went to build it. It was already about 90% there: `FAST_SEASONS` holds
six seasons (Ramadan, Lent, Uposatha, Navratri, a self-set eating window, and a fast someone sets
themselves), each with its own `why`, `nourish`, `train` and `fight` counsel; `fastSeasonNow()`
auto-detects Ramadan from Umm al-Qura, Lent from computus and Uposatha from the moon, each labelled
with its own honest margin of error; and `lifeStateBrief()` already tells the model **"This is a
sacred/chosen discipline, NOT a diet — do not nag about an empty daytime food diary."**

That instruction is the whole finding. **The AI was told. The screens never were.**
`fastSeasonNow()` had exactly two consumers: the brief, and its own card in another tab.

What that cost, in the most sensitive surface the nutrition side has:

- **`_nourishConcern()`** flags three or more days under 1,200/1,500 kcal in seven as possible
  disordered eating and offers the Butterfly Foundation. **Navratri is nine days of fruit, milk and
  nuts. Uposatha is no solid food after midday. Ramadan logged honestly is suhoor plus iftar** — a
  500 kcal suhoor and a 900 kcal iftar is 1,400, under the floor, on a completely ordinary day. A
  person keeping their own tradition's fast, logging honestly, was being told that counting may have
  become something they cannot put down.
- **`brotherSpeaks('calorieOver')`** said *"the day getting away from you"* to someone breaking a
  fast with a large iftar — in a branch that already makes exactly this room for the luteal phase
  and for a hard training week.
- **`_gentleCounsel()`** said *"if you're hungry, eat"* at 20:00 to someone keeping Uposatha.

### The fix is NOT to silence the check

A fast is a documented cover for a restricting eating disorder, so suppressing the card during one
would weaken the exact path rule 6 says may never be weakened. What a fast changes is what the
evidence **means**: when most of the low days fall inside a declared season, the clinical reading is
confounded. So the card still appears and still carries both doors to a human — it just speaks in
the tradition's own voice, through a new `mercy` field on each season, about who that tradition has
never bound. Four traditions name a real person to ask (an imam, a priest, a monastic, someone at
your temple); the two a person sets for themselves name nobody, because there is nobody, and
inventing one would be the app pretending an authority exists. **Every line reports what the
tradition holds. None of them rules.**

A minority of fasted days does not reframe the rest (`fasted*2 >= low`), because one Uposatha day
does not explain four other low days and treating it as if it did would hide the real thing.

### Two things this cost that are worth writing down

**`fastSeasonNow()` could only answer about today.** Asking "is a season live now" says nothing
about the day the food was logged, so Ramadan's own low days would have tripped the clinical card
the morning after Eid. It takes an optional timestamp now; every existing caller passes nothing.

**My own ordering test could not fail.** It compared `indexOf('fastSeasonNow')` to decide whether the
fast branch was asked before the cycle branch — but that finds the `let _fsn = …` declaration, which
sits above both branches whichever order they run in. Swapping the branches for real left the suite
green. Anchor on the BRANCH, not on the call. (Seven fault injections in all; two others first came
back as a CRASH rather than a failure — one was an invalid injection, one was a test dereferencing a
null instead of reporting, which is rule 2(n) again.)

Reach, measured rather than assumed: the concern card renders for **everyone**, the counsel line only
in numbers-off mode, and `calorieOver` only when numbers are on — so the latter two cover opposite
populations rather than the same one twice.
