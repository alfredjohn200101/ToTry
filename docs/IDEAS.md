# The idea list — 26 generated, 18 judged, what survived and what convergence says

> *"more things we can do with totry that others are yet to do in this regard that is beneficial for
> self improvement and would never otherwise see in a free app."*

Six lenses, blind to each other — **anti-app**, **longitudinal**, **moment**, **professional**,
**threads**, **underserved**. Each produced 4–5 ideas; my harness took only the top 3 per lens to
refutation, so **nine were never judged at all**. That cap is recorded here rather than hidden,
because two of the eight are among the strongest on the page.

Every judged idea met refuters told to **default to refuted**. One survived. That is the harness
working — but a kill from a hostile refuter is evidence about *the argument as written*, not proof
the idea is worthless, which is why the killed ones are printed in full below.

## Killed — 16

### anti-app
- **The Handover** *(medium)* — when the app can see exactly which hour you are in danger, it tries to put a real person in that hour instead of itself, then stops buzzing you, because someone better is on it.
- **The Note You Take With You** *(large)* — one page written for the actual human you are about to sit in front of, so the eight minutes you get with them are not spent trying to remember.
- **The Redundancy Audit** *(small)* — every reminder gets asked, out loud, whether it is still doing any work; the ones that are not get switched off, with the evidence shown.

### longitudinal
- **What Left Without You Noticing** *(small)* — a quiet census, once a season, of practices that were genuinely part of your life and have silently stopped, with three answers, one of which is to let it go honestly and never hear about it again.
- **The Ignition** *(medium)* — watches for the days your good stretches **began** (not what they contained) and tells you what happened in the week before each one — nearly always on a completely different front from the stretch itself.

### moment
- **The Stacked Day** *(medium)* — on the two or three days a month when your whole life is stacked against you it comes earlier and softer, and on every ordinary day it says nothing at all.
- **The Hour After** *(small)* — ninety minutes after a slip, because the damage was never the slip; it is the *"I have broken it anyway"* that arrives while nobody is looking.
- **What Actually Works On You** *(medium)* — stops handing you the next technique in the list and starts handing you the one you have actually come back from.

### professional
- **Your Rule (a rule of life)** *(medium)* — once a season you write six short lines about the life you actually want, across body, prayer, people, money and rest; the app never asks you about them again because it already knows, and at the season's end shows which four became how you live.
- **The Handover — the page you take to a human** *(medium)* — one page of your own organised evidence (sleep, mood, weight, drinking, cycle, or income and outgoings) for a GP, a counsellor or a free debt adviser.
- **The fast that knows your body and your tradition** *(small)* — through Ramadan, Lent, Navratri, Uposatha or a fast a person sets themselves, it places protein, water, training and sleep around the real times of that day, and says honestly when their own tradition says they are not obliged.

### threads
- **Outgrown** *(small)* — switches its own features off once you have visibly stopped needing them, tells you it did, and keeps a list of what it handed back.
- **Protein on what's left** *(medium)* — the week's meal plan built to the money actually in the grocery budget, naming the cheapest real protein on your own list.

### underserved
- **The Wake-Anchored Day** *(large)* — stops assuming your day starts in the morning; learns when you actually wake and anchors everything to that, so a night nurse's 7am dinner is dinner.
- **What's In The House** *(medium)* — for someone with no money left, the only question that matters: what can I actually eat between now and payday, out of what is already in the kitchen plus what little is left — and honestly when the answer is *"this isn't enough, here is real help."*
- **Given Days** *(small)* — when someone comes back after a stretch away, it asks once what the days **were**, and if they went on someone else, on a loss, or on being ill, it re-reads its own history so those days count as **given**, not missed.

## What the list says that no single idea does

**Three lenses, blind to each other, produced the same idea: a page you hand to a real human.**
"Something To Take With You", "The Note You Take With You" and "The Handover — the page you take to
a human" are one feature described three times. Two of the three were killed and one survived, which
is a fact about how each argument was written, not about the idea. Convergence across independent
generators is stronger evidence than any single refuter's verdict.

**Two lenses produced the same fast.** "The fast that knows your body and your tradition" and "The
fast is a body too" are the same feature. The app already has the `FAITHS` registry and already
holds the body; the gap is that the nutrition side currently *argues* with a fast instead of serving
it.

Both point at the two things this app can do that nothing free does:
1. **It already knows your tradition** — so a fast, a season and a rule of life are native here and
   nowhere else.
2. **It already refuses to be the endpoint** — so "here is the page, go and see a human" is the one
   feature an engagement-funded app structurally cannot ship.

## The recurring reasons things were killed

Worth reading before building any of them, because every one of these is a real constraint:

1. **It needs data nobody has yet.** Most of the longitudinal and moment ideas need months of
   history. `THE-GAP.md` carries the same caveat about `fromPlan`.
2. **It is a judgement wearing a measurement's clothes.** "What actually works on you" needs a
   causal claim the data cannot support — the person who came back may have come back anyway.
3. **It can shame.** "You have stopped praying" is true, cheap to compute, and against the soul of
   the app.
4. **It duplicates something already built.** Several were versions of surfaces that exist.

## BUILT, 30 Sep 2026 — and it was a defect, not a feature

I ranked the fast first and went to build it. It was already about 90% there: `FAST_SEASONS` holds
six seasons (Ramadan, Lent, Uposatha, Navratri, a self-set eating window, and a fast someone sets
themselves), each with its own `why`, `nourish`, `train` and `fight` counsel; `fastSeasonNow()`
auto-detects Ramadan from Umm al-Qura, Lent from computus and Uposatha from the moon, each labelled
with its own honest margin of error; and `lifeStateBrief()` already tells the model **"This is a
sacred/chosen discipline, NOT a diet — do not nag about an empty daytime food diary."**

That instruction is the whole finding. **The AI was told. The screens never were.**
`fastSeasonNow()` had exactly two consumers: the brief, and its own card in another tab.

What that cost, in the most sensitive surface the nutrition side has:

- **`_nourishConcern()`** flags three or more days under 1,200/1,500 kcal in seven as possible
  disordered eating and offers the Butterfly Foundation. **Navratri is nine days of fruit, milk and
  nuts. Uposatha is no solid food after midday. Ramadan logged honestly is suhoor plus iftar** — a
  500 kcal suhoor and a 900 kcal iftar is 1,400, under the floor, on a completely ordinary day. A
  person keeping their own tradition's fast, logging honestly, was being told that counting may have
  become something they cannot put down.
- **`brotherSpeaks('calorieOver')`** said *"the day getting away from you"* to someone breaking a
  fast with a large iftar — in a branch that already makes exactly this room for the luteal phase
  and for a hard training week.
- **`_gentleCounsel()`** said *"if you're hungry, eat"* at 20:00 to someone keeping Uposatha.

### The fix is NOT to silence the check

A fast is a documented cover for a restricting eating disorder, so suppressing the card during one
would weaken the exact path rule 6 says may never be weakened. What a fast changes is what the
evidence **means**: when most of the low days fall inside a declared season, the clinical reading is
confounded. So the card still appears and still carries both doors to a human — it just speaks in
the tradition's own voice, through a new `mercy` field on each season, about who that tradition has
never bound. Four traditions name a real person to ask (an imam, a priest, a monastic, someone at
your temple); the two a person sets for themselves name nobody, because there is nobody, and
inventing one would be the app pretending an authority exists. **Every line reports what the
tradition holds. None of them rules.**

A minority of fasted days does not reframe the rest (`fasted*2 >= low`), because one Uposatha day
does not explain four other low days and treating it as if it did would hide the real thing.

### Two things this cost that are worth writing down

**`fastSeasonNow()` could only answer about today.** Asking "is a season live now" says nothing
about the day the food was logged, so Ramadan's own low days would have tripped the clinical card
the morning after Eid. It takes an optional timestamp now; every existing caller passes nothing.

**My own ordering test could not fail.** It compared `indexOf('fastSeasonNow')` to decide whether the
fast branch was asked before the cycle branch — but that finds the `let _fsn = …` declaration, which
sits above both branches whichever order they run in. Swapping the branches for real left the suite
green. Anchor on the BRANCH, not on the call. (Seven fault injections in all; two others first came
back as a CRASH rather than a failure — one was an invalid injection, one was a test dereferencing a
null instead of reporting, which is rule 2(n) again.)

Reach, measured rather than assumed: the concern card renders for **everyone**, the counsel line only
in numbers-off mode, and `calorieOver` only when numbers are on — so the latter two cover opposite
populations rather than the same one twice.

## Where the interest actually is

If it were mine to order — cheapest and most on-thesis first:

1. **The fast** — the registry exists, the body exists, and nothing else free does it for five
   traditions.
2. **Given Days** — small, and it is grace-over-shame expressed as arithmetic rather than as copy.
3. **When I'm The Scroll** — small, and it is anti-engagement made literal.
4. **The page you take to a human** — the convergent idea, and the one the soul document already
   names as the point ("it points beyond itself").
5. **What's In The House** — the hardest to get right and the one that would matter most to the
   person who most needs a free app.

Everything below that needs data that starts accumulating today, and I would rather say so than
build something that reads well against a fixture and says nothing true to a real person in March.
