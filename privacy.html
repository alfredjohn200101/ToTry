Privacy Policy

By Alfred John · Last updated July 2026 · Plain English

This is a one-person project, not a corporation. I (Alfred) built this and I run it. I will not pretend the app does more privacy work than it does. Here is exactly what happens to your data.

1. What is collected

Your email (to sign in), and everything you log inside the app: vices, habits, journal entries, training, nutrition, finances, prayers, goals, reflections, scripture, examens, measurements. Plus an anonymous identifier that links your data to your sign-in.

2. Where it lives

Two places. (a) On your device, in local storage. (b) In a Supabase database hosted on AWS in Sydney, Australia. Supabase encrypts data at rest. I (Alfred), as the project owner, can technically read this database — I have not, and I don't intend to, but it is not zero-knowledge encryption. If that matters to you, you should know it.

3. What gets sent to third parties

Some app features call external services to work. Each one sees something specific:

  • Coach & AI features — your message plus relevant context (recent state from your logs) goes to whichever AI provider responds first: Google (Gemini), Groq (Llama), OpenRouter, or Anthropic. These companies have their own privacy policies. Many providers retain prompts for safety review for around 30 days, and some may use prompts to improve models unless an enterprise tier is used. I use standard API tiers — assume your Coach messages may be reviewed by automated systems and retained briefly by the provider.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) — on iPhone, only if you grant permission, the app reads six health metrics — steps, active energy, workouts, heart rate, mindful minutes and sleep — so your movement, training and rest count toward your day automatically, computed on your device. Unlike the other items in this list, your Health data is not sent to any third party: it is not uploaded to my database, not sent to any AI provider, not used for advertising, and never shared or sold. Anything written back to Apple Health (like a logged workout) is written only at your request. You can revoke access anytime in iOS Settings → Health.
  • Workout screenshot / meal photo reading — the image is sent to Google Gemini Vision to read the numbers.
  • Barcode scanning — the barcode is sent to OpenFoodFacts (open database).
  • USDA food search — the search query is sent to api.nal.usda.gov.
  • Exercise info — an exercise name is sent to ExerciseDB / Wger.
  • Currency rates — your selected currency is sent to api.frankfurter.dev.
  • Strava — if you connect it, Strava serves your activities via OAuth you explicitly authorise.
  • Hevy — if you enter an API key, Hevy sees your sync requests. You explicitly authorise this.
  • Bible verse lookups — verse references go to api.esv.org.

4. What never leaves your device

Progress photos. They are stored only in local storage. They are not uploaded to my database, not synced across devices, not sent to any AI provider. If you clear the app's data, they're gone.

Your cycle data, if you track it. Period dates, flow and symptoms are stored in local storage on your device only. They are not written to my database and not synced. They are never sent to any AI provider — with one exception you control: if you switch on Settings → Your cycle → Let the coach see my phase (off by default), the coach is told the phase word only (for example “luteal”), never your dates, flow or symptoms. Leave it off and nothing about your cycle ever leaves the device. Backing them up to your account is off by default and only happens if you switch it on yourself in Settings → Your cycle — which also has a one-tap delete that erases them here and on the server. I will not sell, share or hand this data to anyone, and while backup is off there is nothing on my server for anyone to ask me for.

5. What I do not do

I do not run ads. I do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. No third party pays me to feature their service. There is no advertising or profiling SDK in this app.

Two things do count usage, and you should know exactly what they are:

  • A visitor counter (GoatCounter) — records page views and referrers. Cookie-free, no personal data, no cross-site tracking, no profile of you.
  • Anonymous feature counts — stored in my own Supabase database against a random ID that is not your email: which features got used, with no content attached. For example “app opened”, “reminder set”, “import run”, “a breathing exercise was done”, “an evening reflection was completed”, plus whether the app was installed to your home screen and a truncated technical error message when something crashes.

Neither ever contains what you log. Your vices, prayers, journal entries, food, money and body data are never sent to either one. The counts record that a feature was used, never what you put into it: not the feeling you picked, not the vice, not your stage, not your day rating, not the name you gave anything. I removed the few that carried more than that.

Two things are worth being explicit about, because it would be easy to do them and I have chosen not to: there is no per-person record of whether you slipped or held on, and no attempt to build a picture of an individual from these rows. Watching someone's fight from the outside is the opposite of what this app is for.

If you would rather not be counted at all, switch it off in Settings → Your data — the app behaves exactly the same. Turning it off does not stop a message you send me: if you write to me or answer the progress check-in, that reaches me either way, because you pressed send.

6. What I cannot control

Once your Coach message reaches a provider like Google or Anthropic, that data is governed by their privacy policies, not mine. I have no influence over their model-training practices or retention periods. Treat your Coach messages the way you would treat a message to any cloud service — not a sealed diary.

7. Your control

Export everything (Settings → Your data → Export). Delete your account and all server-side data (Settings → Account → Delete account permanently). Clear local data anytime. You can do any of these without asking me.

8. Children

To Try is not designed for users under 16. I cannot verify age. If you are under 16, please don't use this app without a parent's knowledge.

9. Contact

Email totrybyaj@gmail.com or DM @totry_aj on Instagram with any privacy concern. I respond personally — there is no support team.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top updates and the change goes in-app. I will not silently expand what I do with your data.
