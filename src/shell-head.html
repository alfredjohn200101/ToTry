Your cycle
Log the day your period starts and the rest of the app stops reading your month as one flat line — your food, your training, your fight and your sleep all get the context. It’s an estimate from your own logs, never a certainty, and never fertility or contraception guidance.
Where it lives: on this device only, by default. It is not sent to my server, never sent to any AI provider as dates, and never sold or shared — there is no advertiser or data broker anywhere in this app. If anyone ever asks me for your cycle data, there is nothing on my server to hand over unless you switched backup on yourself. Privacy & legal
Your data lives in two places: on this device, and in your account so it follows you if you change or lose your phone. A few things stay on the device only — your progress photos, and your cycle data unless you switch backup on yourself. When you use AI features (Coach, scripture, screenshot reading), your request and relevant context are sent to an AI provider (Google, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Cloudflare or Anthropic) to generate a reply; their handling is governed by their own policies. ToTry does not sell your data and runs no ads. Please read the full privacy policy for details.
